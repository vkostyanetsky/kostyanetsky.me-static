Completely Lazy
12 September 2014 ·
Family

My daughter asks: Dad, what does “pray” mean?

I explain: well, some people believe in a powerful being who lives in the sky and controls absolutely everything. From time to time, people ask this being for things, and that’s called prayer.

The little one thinks for a moment:

“Dad, are those people just really lazy?”
